01 / SUBMISSION

Submission instructions

Only full papers are accepted, maximum 7 pages including figures, tables, references and all other material. Submissions must be original, unpublished, not under review elsewhere, in English, and in the IEEE two-column conference format. Submit via EDAS ↗

02 / CAMERA-READY

Final manuscript

Camera-ready submission is due February 7, 2027. Accepted and presented papers will be included in the ICIN 2027 conference proceedings.

03 / PRESENTATION

At the workshop

The planned workshop format includes technical research sessions and cross-domain discussion. Confirmed presentation details will follow the final program.

04 / SCIENTIFIC FOCUS

Evidence from applications

Explain the specific task, required information, transmitted representation, application-level performance and system costs. An abstract semantic treatment alone is not enough.

Related links
