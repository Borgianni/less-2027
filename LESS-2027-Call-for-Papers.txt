CALL FOR PAPERS
1st Workshop on Learning to Extract and Send Semantics (LESS 2027)
Co-located with the 30th Conference on Innovation in Clouds, Internet and Networks (ICIN 2027)
Pisa, Italy — 15 March 2027
Website: https://borgianni.github.io/less-2027/

SCOPE
Networked intelligent systems increasingly process continuous and heterogeneous data generated by sensors, wearable devices, biomedical platforms, microphones, cameras, radar systems, and industrial equipment. In many applications, the receiving entity does not require the complete source data, but a representation containing sufficient information for a specific downstream task.

LESS 2027 focuses on the extraction, representation, communication, and evaluation of task-sufficient application-level information derived from real-world data streams. The workshop considers the complete chain from physical observations and source data to semantic representations, network communication, and downstream application decisions. It brings together semantic, goal-oriented and task-oriented communication, application-aware sensing, Edge AI, distributed inference and experimental networked systems.

APPLICATION-GROUNDED EVALUATION REQUIREMENT
Every submission MUST be grounded in a concrete and credible application scenario. Authors are expected to identify (1) the original source data or observations; (2) the downstream application and its information requirements; (3) the semantic representation extracted; (4) information communicated and omitted; (5) impact on application-level performance; and (6) impact on communication or computing-system operation. A use case presented only as motivation is NOT sufficient. Physical deployments are encouraged but not required; experimental datasets and traces, prototypes, testbeds, emulation, digital twins and realistic implementations are appropriate when they support application-level evaluation.

TOPICS OF INTEREST
• Application-grounded semantic communication; application-level semantic extraction
• Task-sufficient semantic representations; goal-oriented and task-oriented communication
• Semantic interfaces between applications and networks; representation of application information requirements
• Semantic extraction from continuous sensor streams
• Event-, state-, intent-, token-, feature- and embedding-based communication
• Multimodal semantic representations; semantic filtering and relevance-aware communication
• Edge AI for semantic extraction; device-edge-cloud semantic processing
• Distributed and split inference; application-aware and semantic-aware networking
• Semantic freshness and Value of Information
• Uncertainty and confidence in semantic representations
• Adaptive semantic representations and semantic update generation
• Semantic sufficiency metrics and interoperability
• Processing and prioritization of semantic traffic
• Mechanisms for requesting additional or source-level information
• Benchmarks for application utility versus communicated information
• Experimental systems, datasets, testbeds and real-world deployments

Application areas include digital health, wearable systems, sports sensing, speech and audio, industrial IoT, robotics, smart environments, intelligent transportation, autonomous systems and integrated sensing and communication.
