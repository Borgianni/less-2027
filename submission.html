Full papers only · Maximum 7 pages

LESS 2027 accepts only full papers, up to a maximum of seven (7) pages. There are no short-paper or demo-paper tracks. Papers will be submitted through the LESS 2027 EDAS subconference of ICIN 2027. The workshop-specific EDAS link and final manuscript-format instructions will be published once the subconference is configured and approved.

Core submission requirement

The application must be concrete.

LESS does not seek generic discussion of semantic models without evidence at the application level. A use case only mentioned for motivation is not sufficient. Authors are expected to address each of the following:

  1. 01 Source observationsIdentify the underlying physical measurements, signals, traces or original data.
  2. 02 Application & requirementsDefine a concrete downstream task and which information it requires.
  3. 03 Extracted representationExplain how task-relevant events, states, features or other representations are derived.
  4. 04 Communicated vs. omittedMake explicit what is sent and which source-level information is not transmitted.
  5. 05 Application-level performanceEvaluate task success and quality using the communicated representation.
  6. 06 System-level impactMeasure the consequences for communication, computation and their trade-offs.

Physical deployments are encouraged but not mandatory. Real datasets, traces, prototypes, testbeds, emulation and realistic system models are welcome when application-level evaluation is credible.
