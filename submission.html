All submissions must be written in English and use the standard IEEE two-column conference template. The 7-page maximum includes figures, tables, references, and all other material. Only full papers are accepted.

01 / Requirements

Preparing your submission

Authors are invited to submit original, unpublished contributions that are not under review elsewhere. Submissions must relate to the workshop's scope and will be peer-reviewed for originality, technical quality, clarity, relevance to the workshop, and the strength of their application-grounded evaluation.

  • Language English.
  • Format Standard IEEE two-column conference template.
  • Maximum length 7 pages, including figures, tables, references, and all other material.
  • Review Peer review on originality, technical quality, clarity, relevance, and application-grounded evaluation.
  • Proceedings Accepted and presented papers will be included in the ICIN 2027 conference proceedings.
