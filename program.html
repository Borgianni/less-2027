Proposed format

Half-day technical workshop

The workshop is planned as a technical forum for research talks and interdisciplinary discussion. The following is a tentative format rather than an announced timetable. Exact slots and invited speakers will be confirmed in coordination with ICIN 2027.

  1. 01 Opening and introduction
  2. 02 Invited keynote (planned)
  3. 03 Technical Session I
  4. 04 Coffee break
  5. 05 Technical Session II
  6. 06 Cross-domain technical discussion
  7. 07 Closing panel

Workshop day: 15 March 2027 in Pisa, as published by ICIN 2027. The detailed program remains to be announced.
